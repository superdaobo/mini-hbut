## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a minimal, refined, academic-luxury experience tailored for higher education faculty. The design balances precision, effortless scannability, and dignified clarity. By rejecting visual clutter and informational noise, it empowers educators to digest schedules, workload quotas, student attendance, and administrative workflows in seconds.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with **Modern Tactile & Micro-depth** cues:
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Pure porcelain canvases paired with clear sky tints and hyper-precise accent signals.
- **Micro-Depth & Soft Architecture**: Structured around spacious, pillowed white cards (18px–22px curvature) lifted gently by multi-stage ambient diffusion and subtle internal specular reflections.
- **Dynamic Breathing & Staggering**: Data points, attendance matrices, and metric badges arrive through gentle staggered cascades with soft ambient glows, evoking institutional stability and digital fluidity.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a crisp hierarchy designed to communicate status, magnitude, and state without descriptive text.

### Brand & Interactive Colors
- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Anchor color for key visual targets, primary triggers, completed milestone indicators, and peak workload highlights.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6`)**: Active navigation controls, vibrant metric ring progress, and high-emphasis focus rings.
- **Tertiary / Warning (`#F59E0B`)**: Alert accents, conflict indicators, pending approvals, and underperforming threshold flags.

### Canvas & Neutral Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft cool base that prevents harsh contrasts against dark text.
- **Surface Highlight (`#F0F7FF`)**: Tonal accent for "Today" grid timelines, dynamic calendar columns, and subtle card gradients.
- **Typography Neutrals**: Deep slate (`#0F172A`) for primary metric hero digits; graphite (`#475569`) for subtitles and labels; soft silver (`#94A3B8`) for secondary tags and micro units. Small pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text is strictly forbidden against low-contrast backgrounds.

### Categorical & Visualization Palette
- **Course Identity Tokens**: Lake Blue (`#0284C7`), Wisteria (`#8B5CF6`), Teal (`#0D9488`), Amber (`#D97706`), and Coral (`#F43F5E`) distinguish academic subjects and student avatar chips across compact tables.

## Typography
The typography system prioritizes immediate scannability of key metrics and structured academic metadata.

### Quantitative Numerical Hierarchy
Numerical points are treated as primary graphic elements rather than plain text. 
- Large metrics (`display-kpi` and `metric-hero`) use tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to establish visual gravity.
- Numeric metrics pair with subordinate secondary unit labels (`14px` or `13px`) positioned along the baseline.
- Micro tags (`9px` to `10px`) manage compact schedule cells and multi-column comparison summaries with clamped lines to eliminate text overflow.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid responsive canvas optimized for compact mobile viewports while adapting smoothly to tablet and desktop dashboards.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile Viewport (<768px)**: Single column stacked with `16px` outer horizontal margins. Internal components use a 4-column sub-grid for operational service launchers, and a 3-column sub-grid for key academic counters.
- **Weekly Schedule Grid**: 40px fixed vertical timestamp axis paired with 7 fluid daily columns (Monday–Sunday) and 11 academic session blocks.
- **Tablet & Split-View (≥768px)**: Reflows to a structured dual-column layout with 24px gutters, separating schedule calendars from operational status boards.

### Spacing Philosophy
Empty vertical space functions as structural grouping. Visual breathing room is preserved by keeping minimum interior card padding at `14px`–`18px` while compressing secondary metrics and metadata grids to `4px`–`8px` item gaps.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system is conveyed through layered tonal planes and diffused light refraction rather than sharp cast shadows.

### Surface Tiers & Specular Depth
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC` flat canvas backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a continuous border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Soft diffuse shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Floating Layers)**: Bottom-docked status indicators, week navigation pills, and dynamic modals utilize frosted glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background tint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, and elevation shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`.

### Breathing Glow & Ambient Highlights
Critical status accents emit soft directional ambient light. Conflict tags (`#F59E0B`) carry a `0 0 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)` ambient highlight, while primary active nodes project a subtle blue halo (`0 4px 14px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.3)`).

## Shapes
The shape language uses pronounced, tactile curves across all functional layers.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Cards & Modules**: `18px` to `22px` for main operational cards, metric groups, and major canvas panels.
- **Schedule Event Blocks**: `12px` continuous corners for compact calendar items.
- **Micro Avatars**: `13px` rounded squircle for student identification tiles (`40px × 40px`).
- **Interactive Controls & Status Badges**: Full pill shape (`9999px`) for search triggers, week selector pills, filter tabs, and segmented step nodes.
- **Micro Primitives**: `3px` for horizontal progress caps and workload chart bars; `2px` for attendance matrix heat tiles.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, pure white text (`14px`, 600 weight), pill-rounded (`9999px`), internal padding `10px 20px`. Subtle diffuse projection: `0 4px 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill**: Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`, text `#2563EB`, zero border, active tap feedback opacity of `0.75`.

### Cards & Container Modules
- **Metric Card**: Multi-tier white card (`rounded-20px`) with a delicate inset specular glow. Contains a 28px–32px numeric display, sub-unit text, and micro progress bar (`6px` height) with rounded caps.
- **Schedule Course Tile**: Tinted container drawn from the course palette with a 15% opacity fill, matching 100% solid text, `12px` radius, and an optional 3px solid left status rail for schedule adjustments or class changes.

### Badges, Chips & Progress Indicators
- **Attendance & Metric Rings**: `44px` outer diameter with a `4px` circular track stroke (`#E2E8F0`), overlaid with a dynamic primary or amber gradient fill. Centered microcopy uses `10px` bold counters (`61/61`).
- **Status Indicator Badges**: Pill-shaped capsules (`18px`–`22px` height) with soft tinted fills (`#ECFDF5` for normal, `#FEF2F2` for warnings) paired with `11px` bold text and an inline 6px dot indicator.

### Input & Search Fields
- Fully rounded (`9999px`) pill inputs with a `#FFFFFF` base, a `1px` subtle outline (`#E2E8F0`), and an inset search glyph. Includes a `300ms` debounce threshold for class and roster queries.

### Empty States & Low-Density Modules
- Vacant metrics collapse into a single unified summary card rather than stacking multiple empty panels. Content is presented in a muted horizontal line (`13px`, `#64748B`) paired with a minimal monochrome icon to maintain visual rhythm.
